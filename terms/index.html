5. Booking & consultations

  • Booking: choose a doctor and an available time, then pay to confirm. The slot is held for 15 minutes while you pay. If payment isn't completed, the slot is released.
  • Being on time: be available at the booked time with a working internet connection, camera and microphone allowed, and notifications on. The doctor calls you through the app.
  • Sharing information: give the doctor accurate, complete information about your health. Documents you attach to a booking are shared with that doctor.
  • Quick Connect: you can request an immediate consultation with an available doctor. The doctor has 30 minutes to accept, and then 30 minutes to call you.
  • Prescriptions: a doctor issues a prescription only if they decide it is clinically appropriate and allowed for teleconsultation. Getting a prescription is not guaranteed.

6. Fees & payments

  • What you pay: each doctor sets their own consultation fee. DoCall adds a platform fee and applicable GST. You see the full breakdown, including any coupon discount, before you pay.
  • How you pay: payments are processed by our payment partner, Cashfree Payments. DoCall never sees or stores your card, UPI or net-banking credentials.
  • Payment problems: if money is debited but the booking isn't confirmed, it is usually updated within a few minutes. If it isn't, contact Support. Any amount not linked to a confirmed booking is refunded to your original payment method.
  • Coupons: coupons have their own conditions. They can't be exchanged for cash, and we may withdraw a coupon that is misused.

7. Cancellations & refunds

If you cancel

  • You can cancel a booked consultation in the app before it starts.
  • The refund is a percentage of the consultation fee, and depends on how long before the appointment you cancel. The earlier you cancel, the more you get back. The current percentages are shown in the app, and the exact refund amount is shown before you confirm the cancellation.
  • The platform fee and GST are not refundable when you cancel. The non-refunded part of the consultation fee goes to the doctor, whose time was reserved for you.

Quick Connect

If the doctor declines, doesn't respond within 30 minutes, or doesn't call within 30 minutes of accepting, the request expires and you receive a full refund automatically.

If the doctor can't attend

If your doctor cancels or doesn't join a confirmed consultation, contact Support. We will help you rebook or arrange a refund.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Cashfree. How long it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider. You can follow the refund status on the appointment.
