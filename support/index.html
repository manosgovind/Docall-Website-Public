Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

How do I sign in?

Enter your mobile number and the 6-digit code we send you by SMS. If you've signed in on the same phone before, you can use Face ID or your fingerprint instead. If the code doesn't arrive, wait a minute and tap Resend, and check that the number is correct.

How do I book a consultation?
  1. Find a doctor by name, speciality or location.
  2. Choose a date and an available time.
  3. Add the reason for your visit, and any reports you want the doctor to see.
  4. Pay to confirm. Your slot is held for 15 minutes while you complete the payment.

You'll get a confirmation, plus a reminder shortly before the consultation.

My money was debited but the booking isn't confirmed

Payment confirmations from the bank can take a few minutes, and the booking updates on its own once the payment is confirmed. If it still isn't confirmed after 30 minutes, contact us with your registered mobile number and the time of payment. Any amount that didn't result in a confirmed booking is refunded to your original payment method.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

Open the appointment and tap Cancel. The refund depends on how long before the appointment you cancel, and the app shows the exact refund amount before you confirm. Refunds go back to your original payment method, and you can follow their status on the appointment.

What is Quick Connect?

Quick Connect lets you request a video consultation with an available doctor right away. The doctor has 30 minutes to accept, and once they accept, another 30 minutes to call you. If the doctor declines, doesn't respond or doesn't call in time, the request expires and you get a full refund automatically.

The video or audio call isn't working
  • Allow DoCall to use your camera and microphone in your phone's settings.
  • Use a stable Wi-Fi or 4G/5G connection, and close other apps using the camera.
  • Turn on notifications so you don't miss the doctor's call. On Android, also allow DoCall to show calls on the lock screen and to run in the background.
  • If you miss the call, you'll get a notification, and the doctor can call you again.
I'm a doctor. How do I join DoCall?

Download the app, tap Sign up and choose I'm a Doctor, then add your qualifications, medical registration details, consultation fees and verification documents. Our team verifies your registration before your profile is shown to patients. You can then set your availability and share your personal invite QR code with patients.

When do doctors get paid?

Earnings from completed consultations are paid to the bank account in your payment settings, on DoCall's payout schedule. You can follow every payout in the app. After your first payout, your bank details are locked to protect your earnings. To change them, contact support.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.
  2. Review what will be deleted and what we have to keep.
  3. Confirm with the code we send to your registered mobile number.

If you have upcoming appointments, a pending refund or a payout in progress, the app asks you to wait until they're complete. Details are in our Privacy Policy. If you can't access the app, email us from your registered email address.

How is my health information protected?

Your medical documents are stored privately and open only through short-lived, access-checked links. Only you and the doctor of the appointment you share them with can see them. We never sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy, or contact us to access or correct your data.
