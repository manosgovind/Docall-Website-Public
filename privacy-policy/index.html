1. Who we are

DoCall is a digital health platform operated by Coddeit Corp ("DoCall", "we", "us"). It lets patients find verified doctors, book and pay for consultations, and consult by video, audio or chat. Doctors use DoCall to manage their availability, consult with patients and receive payouts.

For the personal data described here, Coddeit Corp is the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Doctors remain responsible for the clinical records they create as part of their professional practice.

By using DoCall you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service: create and secure your account, show doctor profiles, book, reschedule and cancel appointments, run video and audio consultations and chat, and share documents between you and your doctor for an appointment.
  • To process payments: collect consultation payments, issue refunds under the cancellation policy, and pay doctors their earnings.
  • To verify doctors: check doctors' registration and qualification documents before they are listed.
  • To keep you informed: booking confirmations, reminders, incoming-call and missed-call alerts, refund updates and account messages by push notification, SMS or email.
  • To keep DoCall safe: verify your phone number, prevent fraud and misuse, enforce our Terms of Service, and investigate problems.
  • To improve DoCall: fix crashes and errors and understand how features perform.
  • To meet legal duties: keep tax, payment and medical records as required by law, and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Marketing: only if you have agreed to it. You can opt out at any time.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you sign up and use the features, and for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows, such as meeting legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time (see Your rights). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that has already happened.

4. Who we share it with

Between patients and doctors

When you book a consultation, the doctor sees your name, age, gender, the reason for your visit and any documents you attach to that appointment. Patients see a doctor's public profile, including name, photo, qualifications, specialisation, experience, clinic and fees. Doctors' bank details and verification documents are never shown to patients.

5. Device permissions

DoCall asks for permissions only when a feature needs them. You can change them at any time in your phone's settings.

  • Camera and microphone: for video and audio consultations, and to photograph documents or your profile picture.
  • Photos and files: to attach reports, prescriptions or images, and to save documents you download.
  • Notifications: for reminders, incoming-call alerts and updates.
  • Face ID / fingerprint (optional): to lock the app and for quick sign-in on a remembered device. This is handled entirely by your phone.

6. How long we keep it

  • While your account is active: we keep your account and health information so you can use DoCall.
  • Records the law requires us to keep: consultation, prescription and payment records are kept for the periods required by Indian tax, payments and medical-records laws, even after you delete your account.
  • Short-lived data: sign-in verification proofs expire within an hour. Doctor-invite click records are deleted after 7 days. Remembered devices expire after 90 days without use.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. For your security, we confirm the request with a one-time code sent to your registered mobile number. Deletion may be paused until upcoming appointments, pending refunds or payouts are completed, so nothing you are owed is lost.

What happens when you delete your account

  • You are signed out on all devices, and the account can no longer be used.
  • Your profile photo, date of birth, gender, uploaded medical documents, saved devices, notifications and push tokens are deleted. Your chat profile is removed.
  • Doctors are removed from DoCall straight away. Patients can no longer find or book them.

What we keep, and why

  • Your name, mobile number and email, so we can contact you if an audit, legal or payment matter needs it.
  • Consultation and payment records that the law requires us to keep.
  • Prescriptions and notes issued by a doctor, which remain part of that doctor's and patient's medical records.
  • Messages already delivered to the other person in a consultation chat. They are shown as sent by a deleted user.
  • For doctors: a secure record of your registration, qualifications, verification documents and payouts, kept for regulatory and financial audits. It is never shown in the app, and only authorised DoCall staff can access it.

Marketing after deletion: we will not send you offers or promotions after you delete your account, unless you choose to allow it on the deletion screen. You can opt out of those messages at any time.

If you can't use the app, you can also ask us to delete your account through Support. We will verify your identity first.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards that are appropriate for health information. They include:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all communication with our servers;
  • sign-in only after verifying your phone number with a one-time code;
  • private storage for medical files, opened only through short-lived links after an access check;
  • sign-in credentials kept in your phone's secure keychain;
  • role-based access, so staff see only what their job needs.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. Keep your phone locked, and never share your sign-in codes with anyone. DoCall staff will never ask for them.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you can:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • Correct or update your details. Most can be edited in the app under Profile;
  • Erase your data by deleting your account (see Deleting your account), subject to the records we must keep by law;
  • Withdraw consent for any processing based on consent, including marketing;
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity;
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied with our response, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

10. Children

You must be 18 or older to create a DoCall account. A parent or legal guardian may book and attend a consultation for a child from their own account. They give consent on the child's behalf and are responsible for the information they share. We do not knowingly create accounts for children or use children's data for tracking or targeted advertising.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DoCall or the law changes. When we do, we update the "Last updated" date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
