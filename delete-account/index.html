1. Delete in the app

This is the quickest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  • Open DoCall and sign in.
  • Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.
  • Review what will be deleted, then confirm with the one-time code sent to your registered mobile number.

If you have an upcoming appointment, a refund being processed, or (for doctors) a payout in progress, the app asks you to wait until it's complete, so nothing you're owed is lost.

2. Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, for example because you no longer have the phone, email us from the email address registered on your account:

To: support-docall@coddeitcorp.com
Subject: Account deletion request
Include: your full name and registered mobile number.

We will verify that the request comes from the account holder before deleting anything, and confirm by email when your account has been deleted.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account is closed, and you are signed out on all devices.
  • Your profile photo, date of birth, gender and other profile details.
  • Medical documents and reports you uploaded.
  • Saved devices, notifications and push tokens, plus your chat profile.
  • For doctors: your listing is removed immediately, and patients can no longer find or book you.

4. What is kept, and why

  • Your name, mobile number and email, so we can contact you if an audit, legal or payment matter needs it.
  • Consultation, prescription and payment records, for as long as Indian tax, payments and medical-records laws require.
  • Messages already delivered to the other person in a consultation chat. They are shown as sent by a deleted user.
  • For doctors: a secure record of registration, qualifications, verification documents and payouts, for regulatory and financial audits. It is never shown in the app.

We don't send marketing after deletion unless you choose to allow it on the deletion screen. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
